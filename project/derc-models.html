DERC-IAM and DERC-P

Archive

◻ links to be added

Draworld builds and maintains two core working tools:

  • DERC-IAM — an integrated energy–economy–climate assessment model with multi-region economic structure.
  • DERC-P — a simulation model of China's power structure at hourly resolution, used for power balance work; the provincial investment and operation optimisation variant (Draworld-P) supplies the price surfaces behind the transparency indices.

Version history and documentation are not public. Add them here when available.
