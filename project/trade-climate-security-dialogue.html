Trade–Climate–Security Policy Dialogue

2024

贸易—气候—安全政策对话(2025–2030)

Events and outreach ↗

Draworld opened its Trade–Climate–Security Policy Dialogue for 2025–2030 with a first online session. The salon looked at how competitive interests, sustainable development and geopolitical stability can be balanced in an increasingly polarised landscape, drawing on economists, policy researchers and international-relations specialists.

The opening case was EU–China electric vehicle tariffs: the EU's five-year duties on Chinese EVs as a worked example of trade and climate goals pulling against each other, and of why cooperative solutions matter if trade tension is not to compound.
